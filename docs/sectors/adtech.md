# Ad Tech Sector Brief

As of 2026-11-02. Labels: **[V]** = verified (read in source); **[U]** = unverified (industry convention or not read in a source). Derived arithmetic from verified figures is marked **[V, derived]**.

## 1. How money flows

**Open-web programmatic chain:** advertiser -> agency / holding company -> DSP (or ad network) -> exchange / SSP -> mediation (in-app) -> publisher or app developer. [U: standard chain]

- ISBA/PwC UK study (2020): publishers received about 51% of advertiser programmatic spend; 35% was attributed to suppliers (agency, DSP, tech providers, SSP, not itemized); 15% was an unattributable "unknown delta." [V] ([PPA](https://ppa.co.uk/isba-study-reveals-publishers-miss-out-on-half-of-adspend), [Marketing Week](https://www.marketingweek.com/programmatic-advertising-supply-chain-brands/))
- DSP take rate: The Trade Desk reports its take rate has stayed at 19–21% since 2017 (20.3% in 2024). [V] ([AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/online-advertising/as-ctv-blooms-its-knives-out-for-the-trade-desks-take-rate/)) FY2025: revenue $2.896B on $13.4B gross spend, about 21.6%. [V, derived] ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260225420796/en/The-Trade-Desk-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results))
- Amazon DSP and Comcast Universal Ads compete on low or zero DSP fees; SSP take rates "have been squeezed." No SSP percentage sourced. [V] (AdExchanger, above)

**Channel types:**
- **Walled gardens** (Google, Meta, Amazon, TikTok, Apple, Microsoft, Snap): own audience, inventory, buying tools and measurement; advertisers buy direct or through agencies. [U: definition]
- **Retail media:** retailers sell ads using shopper data (Amazon, Walmart Connect, Instacart, Coupang, Criteo as tech partner). [U: definition]
- **CTV:** streaming TV inventory sold direct or programmatically. Magnite CTV was 48% of contribution ex-TAC in Q4 2025. [V] ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/magnites-ctv-bet-pays-off-32-growth-signals-a-streaming-ad-shift/))
- **In-app / mobile gaming:** advertiser (mostly game and app developers doing user acquisition) -> DSP or ad network (AppLovin AXON, Unity Vector, Moloco, Mintegral, Liftoff) -> mediation (AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay) -> app publisher. [V for MAX as mediation: [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/applovin-delivers-1-66b-quarter-as-ai-models-power-66-revenue-surge/); rest U]

## 2. Segments: what demand, supply and unified mean commercially

| Segment | Commercial meaning | Typical customers |
|---|---|---|
| Demand side | Spends advertiser budgets; revenue is a fee or margin on spend | Agencies, brands, app/game marketers [U] |
| Supply side | Sells and yields publisher inventory; revenue is a share of publisher revenue | Publishers, broadcasters, CTV/OEM owners, app developers [U] |
| Unified | One company runs both advertiser buying and publisher monetization (network + mediation/exchange) | Game/app developers on both sides of the same deal [U] |
| Walled garden | Owned-and-operated audience and ad stack | Brands, agencies, SMB self-serve [U] |
| Retail media | Ads on retailer properties and off-site using purchase data | CPG/endemic brands, agencies [U] |
| Measurement/data | Attribution (MMP), verification, identity, panels | Advertisers, agencies, platforms [U] |

Unified examples: AppLovin (AXON ad engine + MAX mediation) [V, PPC Land above]; Unity Grow (Vector ad network + ironSource Ad Network + LevelPlay mediation; Vector was 56% and ironSource Ad Network 11% of Grow revenue in Q4 2025) [V] ([Unity IR](https://investors.unity.com/news/news-details/2026/Unity-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)); Unity–ironSource $4.4B all-stock merger completed November 2022 [V] ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/07/unity-and-ironsources-4-4b-merger-is-now-complete/amp/)).

## 3. Commercial roles by segment [U unless cited]

| Role | What it does | Typical success metrics |
|---|---|---|
| Agency sales / agency partner | Holding-company and agency relationships, upfronts, joint business plans | Quota on booked/billed spend, share of agency wallet |
| Brand / client sales | Direct large-advertiser relationships | Quota, client retention, product adoption |
| Performance / UA sales | App and game marketers buying installs/ROAS | Net revenue, spend growth, ROAS/LTV targets met |
| Publisher / supply partnerships | Sign and grow publishers, apps, CTV owners | Revenue under management, fill rate, eCPM, SDK/mediation share |
| Platform partnerships | Integrations with DSPs, SSPs, OEMs, data and measurement partners | Integrated volume, partner-sourced revenue |
| GM / country or regional head | Full P&L for a market or product line | Revenue, net revenue/margin, headcount efficiency |

- The Trade Desk reports customer retention over 95% for twelve consecutive years, a public example of retention as a headline commercial metric. [V] (BusinessWire, above)
- Walled gardens title senior client roles by client/agency vertical (e.g., Meta "VP, Global Clients and Agencies"; Google "Industry Manager"; Meta "Global Agency Partner"). [V] ([Skift](https://skift.com/2026/03/23/openai-gets-serious-about-chatgpt-ads-hires-former-meta-exec/), [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/ai/openai-hires-google-meta-ad-execs-chatgpt-ads-australia/)) Independents more often use VP/Head of Sales, Partnerships, Supply or Demand. [U]

## 5. Implications for assessing a candidate

**Sourced evidence:**
- OpenAI's first named ad leaders came from walled-garden client/agency sales: Dave Dugan (ex-Meta VP Global Clients and Agencies) to run global ad solutions (Mar 2026); in Australia, a 12-year Google industry manager and a ~10-year Meta global agency partner (Jul 2026). [V] (Skift, Relevant Audience) This supports walled-garden agency/brand sales -> AI-assistant ad sales.
- ChatGPT ads launched through holding companies (WPP, Omnicom, Dentsu) with a $200K minimum, i.e., an agency- and brand-led early sales motion. [V]
- AppLovin and Moloco are moving from mobile gaming UA into e-commerce/web/CTV advertisers. [V] Mobile performance experience is therefore being applied to non-gaming advertisers inside these companies.

**Not sourced (treat as hypotheses):**
- Supply-side mobile monetization -> AI-assistant ads: no source found showing AI-assistant ad businesses hiring from supply-side mobile monetization. [U]
- Demand-side agency sales -> walled-garden brand sales: plausible from title patterns; no hiring-flow data read. [U]
- Supply-side (publisher yield, mediation, SDK share) and demand-side (quota on spend, agency wallet share) use different success metrics; a candidate's metrics should be mapped to the target segment before scoring. [U]
- Unified-platform GM experience (both sides, full P&L) covers more of a target role's scope than single-side experience; no source quantifies this. [U]
